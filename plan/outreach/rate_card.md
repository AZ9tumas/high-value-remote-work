## Fixed offers (USD)

| Offer | Discovery (upfront) | Fixed price |
|---|---|---|
| One moving system, replicated once (recommended) | $150, up to 3 h | $600 |
| One save, duplication or validation bug | $150, up to 3 h | $500 |
| Handling pass for one vehicle | $150, up to 3 h | $750 |
| Larger scoped system | $150, up to 3 h | after discovery, about $1,000 to $1,500 |

Source: `PLAN.md`'s $500 to $1,500 per scoped system, from vendor hiring guides (Memvers, Game-Ace; unverified search summaries). Scope: [`../portfolio.md`](../portfolio.md).

## Hourly

| Type | Rate | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Ad hoc, unscoped | $60/h | prepaid 5-hour blocks; weekly time log |

Sources: senior $50 to $120+/h (Game-Ace, vendor, unverified); freelance Luau $40 to $60/h (Sawhorse, date unverified); full-time contract $30 to $40/h (Creator Games, closed); Upwork $15 to $35/h. Floor: `PLAN.md` stage 3.

## Deposit and milestones

- Under $1,000: 50% deposit, 50% on acceptance.
- $1,000 or more: 40% deposit, 30% at a demo milestone, 30% on acceptance.
- The client runs the agreed check within 5 business days. Video of it passing before the final payment; files and handover after.
- Late payment pauses work. Payment: [METHODS]; check PayPal's Seller Protection terms for your country first.

Source: none for the percentages; milestone pay is common DevForum advice (studio note §4). Full terms: C-008.

## Robux rule

Quote in USD. If a client must pay in Robux, convert at the DevEx rate, $0.0038 per Earned Robux: about **263 Robux per $1**, so $600 is 157,895 Robux. Count Robux as paid only when they leave Pending, and as cash only when DevEx pays.

- Group payouts land in **Pending Robux** first (since 18 Jun 2026; RoUtility News, search summary).
- Group funds count as Earned Robux only if they are bona fide earnings.
- DevEx allows one completed cash-out per calendar month, minimum 30,000 Earned Robux.
- From **1 Nov 2026** DevEx payouts are royalties. Non-US creators with valid tax info: 0 to 30% US withholding on the US-player share. Without it, 24% backup withholding may apply to the whole payout.

Sources: Roblox [DevEx](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/9f840b170b3e472c705e035126b45e3e050daed2/content/en-us/production/monetization/developer-exchange.md) and [tax](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/9f840b170b3e472c705e035126b45e3e050daed2/content/en-us/production/monetization/tax-information.md) docs, read 5 Oct 2026.
