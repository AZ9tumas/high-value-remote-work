## How this was checked

- **Date:** every row was found by web search on 5 Oct 2026.
- **Method:** 50 web searches. I started with the report's studios, then searched ATS boards (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Workable), remote game-job boards and Roblox's own contract roles.
- **Blocked:** the egress proxy refused every job page I tried. That covers WebFetch to twinatlas.com and jobs.paradoxum.gg, and the Ashby, Workable, Greenhouse and Lever APIs. It also covers gamejobs.co, remotegamejobs.com, himalayas.app and uplift.games (one try each). The proxy log also shows DevForum, RoHire, HiddenDevs and jobs.ashbyhq.com refused. GitHub search outside this repo is not enabled.
- **So:** facts come from search-engine excerpts, which can be stale or mixed up. Nothing here is confirmed open.
- **Inferred dates (marked "inferred"):** some job sites and X posts put a timestamp in the listing ID, and DevForum topic numbers rise over time. Two X posts with known dates decoded correctly, so the method works. Still, treat these dates as approximate. An inferred date is when an aggregator first saw a listing, not when it closes.
- **Age:** no lead's excerpt states a minimum age. Check it on the page.

## Before any contact (owner)

1. Open the link. Confirm the role is live and still taking applications.
2. Check country, time zone, age, and whether it is a contract or an employee role.
3. Check the pay, the currency and how they pay.
4. Make sure the page is on the company's own site or ATS. Ignore DMs claiming to be from these studios.
5. Then decide. Contacting, applying or creating an account needs your explicit OK (AGENTS.md rule 2).
