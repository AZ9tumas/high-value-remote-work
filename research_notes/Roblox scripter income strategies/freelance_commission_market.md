## 1. Venues: where experienced Roblox scripters find paid work (Talent Hub, DevForum, Discord servers, X, Fiverr/Upwork/Contra/PPH, agencies)

### Takeaway
Most deal flow is on Discord hiring servers: HiddenDevs has roughly 250k–300k members and RoDevs about 55k. RoHire (rohire.dev) aggregates their posts. The DevForum "Find and Hire Talent" and "Showcase Your Portfolio" categories are active again and carry many 2025–26 posts. Roblox's official Talent Hub exists and requires ID verification, but developers criticise it heavily for low pay, scams and posters who never reply. Fiverr and Upwork carry Roblox gigs at low anchor prices: Fiverr gigs start at $5–$450 and Upwork typically pays $15–$35/hr. Agency and studio contract work exists (for example MELON), but most of the evidence for it is from 2020–21.

### Inferences
- The best balance of volume and price for an expert is likely **HiddenDevs plus the DevForum categories**, with **RoHire as a free tool to monitor several servers at once**. HiddenDevs' skill-role vetting (a working demo, sole credit, GitHub file links) also works as a public credential.
- Talent Hub is worth keeping a profile on because it carries Roblox's ID-verified badge. Evidence suggests it is a **low-signal channel for good-paying work**: complaints about low pay, scams and silent posters keep recurring from 2023 to 2025.
- Fiverr and Upwork bring clients who already think in USD and come with platform escrow, but the visible price anchors are low ($5–$100 starting prices; $15–$35/hr). An expert would need to stand out with niche positioning (for example "live-ops data/monetization systems for existing games") rather than compete on price.
- Brand and agency studios (the MELON model) and established game studios pay in USD on contract terms. These are the most direct route to steady $3k+/month income. Current (2025–26) evidence about specific agencies was not found (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No current figures were found for Talent Hub job volume or pay distribution, or for the job and pay types it currently offers. Its pages and DevForum threads could not be fetched. The latest evidence that it still operates is a DevForum discussion from after Sep 2025.
- I could not confirm whether the Feb 2024 "reopening" of the DevForum Collaboration categories means Talent Hub is being phased out or that the two now run side by side. Only the Fandom wiki makes the claim.
- HiddenDevs hiring-post rules were not found: minimum budgets, whether percentage-only posts are banned, approved payment methods, and any scripter tiers above "Luau Scripter". RoDevs' application or verification tiers and posting rules were not found either.
- No data was found for X/Twitter (#RobloxDev commissions), Contra or PeoplePerHour (volume or prices).
- No 2025–26 evidence was found on subcontracting agencies or collectives (for example Gamefam, Supersocial and similar studios hiring contractors).

## 2. Rates: hourly, per-task, per-system, retainer and revenue-share pricing (USD and Robux)

### Takeaway
Market prices are split in two. Discord and DevForum commissions are dominated by Robux and small budgets: average listings of about $134–$219, and old price guides putting a "system" at 4,500–6,500 R$, or about $17–$25 through DevEx. Pricing aimed at Western clients is far higher: $15–$40/hr for mid-level work, $50–$120+/hr for senior systems engineers, and $1,000–$3,000 (mid) to $3,000–$8,000 (premium) for full systems such as combat, inventory or trading, though those figures come from vendor blogs. Full-time retainers of $3.5k–$5k/month were advertised, but only in 2020–21. Revenue share of 10–45% is common in posts and is widely described as risky.

### Inferences
- Robux prices converted at the current DevEx rate of $0.0038/R$ (my calculation):

  | Robux price | USD via DevEx |
  |---|---|
  | 4,500–6,500 R$ "system" (2021 guide) | ≈ $17–$25 |
  | 25,000–55,000 R$ "large game" | ≈ $95–$209 |
  | 60k R$ (top RoHire scripting listing) | ≈ $228 |
  | 1M R$/month (2021 retainer) | ≈ $3,800 (≈ $3,500 at the 2021 rate of $0.0035) |

  The 2021 "1M R$ / $3,500" offer shows that the post treated Robux and USD as equal **at the DevEx rate**.
- The market has three rough tiers:
  - **Beginner:** $5–$40 per task, or a few thousand R$.
  - **Intermediate:** $15–$40/hr, $50–$550 per task, or 10k–60k R$.
  - **Expert / systems:** $50–$120+/hr; $1k–$8k per full system; full-time $3.5k–$5k/month at the older studio rates.
  
  The expert-tier figures come mostly from vendor blogs and older posts, not from verified 2025–26 transactions.
- A **hybrid deal of upfront payment plus a small percentage** (for example the "9,000 R$ + 30%" style) protects the scripter better than a percentage-only deal.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025–26 survey of scripter rates by skill level was found. No current DevForum price guide was found either (the most-cited one is from 2021).
- Scripting-only averages on RoHire and HiddenDevs were not found; the published averages cover all roles.
- Fiverr's and Upwork's seller or freelancer fee percentages could not be checked because the session's search budget ran out. They are needed to work out net income.
- No sourced 2025–26 data was found on typical retainer rates (for example $X/month for N hours of live-ops support).

## 4. Payment and protection: methods, real Robux value, deposits, scams and contracts

### Takeaway
Being paid in Robux costs heavily in value. Clients buy Robux at about $0.0125/R$, but scripters cash out through DevEx at **$0.0038/R$** (Robux earned since Sep 5, 2025). DevEx has a 30,000 R$ minimum, an age floor of 13+ and US tax forms; it is open to non-US creators via a W-8. Group payouts changed again in **June 2026**: there is no longer a 14-day membership wait, and payouts now land in **Pending Robux**. PayPal Goods & Services gives the buyer a formal dispute path, while Friends & Family offers no protection and can still be abused. Common practice is upfront or partial-upfront payment, proof videos, and keeping files until paid. Account-phishing and "transfer assets first" scams are the main threats documented for 2025–26.

### Cited Findings
**Robux value and DevEx (international)**
- DevEx rate history (third-party repo; verify against Roblox's official page):
  - **$0.0038 per Earned Robux** for Robux earned from **10 AM PT, Sep 5, 2025** onward (+8.5%);
  - **$0.0035** for balances earned before then;
  - 10,000 Earned Robux went from **$35 to $38**.
  
  Sources: [DevForum announcement "Increasing DevEx — Creators Will Now Earn 8.5% More" (Sep 2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-devex-%E2%80%94-creators-will-now-earn-85-more/3920159) (search summary); [GitHub supertj/devex-rates](https://github.com/supertj/devex-rates) (fetched)
- Rate tiers (fetched, third-party):
  - **U.S. 18+ tier: $0.0054/R$, from Jun 8, 2026**, for spending by *age-verified 18+ US players* on developer products, passes, subscriptions and private servers in R15-compliant experiences;
  - legacy $0.0035 balances are **cashed out first**;
  - first cash-out requires a **30,000 Earned Robux** minimum (≈ $114);
  - "Only Earned Robux qualify… Purchased, gifted, or transferred Robux cannot be exchanged."
  
  Sources: [GitHub supertj/devex-rates](https://github.com/supertj/devex-rates); see also [devex.gg](https://devex.gg/) and [DevForum "DevEx rate Confusion" (~2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/devex-rate-confusion/4717348)
- DevEx eligibility:
  - age **13+** (ages 13–17 need a parent or guardian to consent and complete the tax documentation);
  - 30,000 Earned Robux;
  - a verified email;
  - a DevEx portal account (payments run through Tipalti);
  - IRS **W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US)**.
  
  Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs: Developer Exchange](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange); [Roblox Help: DevEx Overview & Requirements](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203314100-Developer-Exchange-DevEx-Overview-How-to-Submit-Requirements); [DevEx Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005718246-Developer-Exchange-Terms-of-Use) (search summary)
- The minimum used to be 50,000 R$ and required Premium; it was reduced to 50,000 with no Premium needed per a Jan 31, 2022 post — [HiddenDevs on X, 2022-01-31](https://x.com/HiddenDevs/status/1488249179046432770). The minimum is now 30,000 per [GitHub devex-rates](https://github.com/supertj/devex-rates)
- Buyers pay roughly **$0.0125 per Robux**, against $0.0038 through DevEx. Examples: a client offering **50,000 R$ ≈ $190**, **100,000 R$ ≈ $380**, **1,000,000 R$ ≈ $3,800** before fees and taxes. "Receiving payment in USD directly would be better" for scripters. RoHire also counts "commissions" among the sources of DevEx-eligible earned Robux — [RoHire Playbook: "Understanding Roblox DevEx: What Your Robux Is Actually Worth"](https://rohire.dev/blog/understanding-roblox-devex-rates) (search summary)
- **Conflict:** RoHire says commission Robux are DevEx-eligible, while the devex-rates repo says "transferred" Robux cannot be exchanged. Group payouts are a transfer mechanism, so official DevEx terms should be checked on whether group-payout Robux count as Earned Robux — [RoHire blog](https://rohire.dev/blog/understanding-roblox-devex-rates) vs [GitHub devex-rates](https://github.com/supertj/devex-rates)

**Group payouts (the standard way Robux commissions are paid)**
- **Feb 11, 2021:** an update blocked recurring or one-time payouts to members who had only recently joined a group — [Bloxy News on X](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/1359950090710839296)
- The rule later became **14 days** of membership, or **3 days for verified groups**, and was applied inconsistently. Some verified groups reported being reverted to 14 days (~late 2025) — [DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/verified-group-no-longer-eligible-for-3-day-payout-wait-time-reverted-to-14-day/4065307); [DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/group-is-verified-but-i-still-have-to-wait-14-days-to-payout-new-members/4117241) (search summary)
- **Oct 22, 2024:** group payouts moved to the Creator Dashboard, and receiving one "will now take **72 Hours of Pending** Wait Time" — [RoNews on X, 2024-10-22](https://x.com/RoGameNews/status/1848600186219565370)
- **Jun 18, 2026:** "You **no longer need to be in a group for 14 days** before receiving a payout (3 days for verified groups). Payouts are now sent directly to the recipient's **Pending Robux**." After a recent 2SV change, Pending Robux may take up to about a month — [RoUtility on X, 2026-06-18](https://x.com/RoUtilityNews/status/2067676523197268027) (search summary for the 2SV detail)
- Flagged payouts can face an extra anti-fraud hold of up to **10 days** — [DevForum "Extra 10 day hold on group payouts" (~2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/extra-10-day-hold-on-group-payouts/4569683). Pending periods were described as about 3–21 days — [Generalist Programmer, "Roblox Group Funds" (2026)](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/roblox-group-funds-explained) (search summary). There are ongoing bug reports of "Group payouts are currently restricted" even for old, funded groups — [DevForum (~2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/group-payouts-are-currently-restricted-older-than-3-weeks-active-group-with-funds/4640314)

**PayPal and other methods**
- PayPal **Goods & Services** is covered by PayPal Purchase Protection, which gives the *buyer* a formal way to get money back. US fees are **2.9% + $0.30**. **Friends & Family** has "no buyer or seller protection" and is free when funded from balance or a bank account. People ask for F&F to avoid refunds, but F&F payments "are not immune to chargebacks and they can even get your PayPal account suspended" — [Wise blog](https://wise.com/us/blog/paypal-friends-and-family-vs-goods-and-services); [PayPal Help: Friends & Family vs Goods & Services](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/whats-the-difference-between-friends-and-family-or-goods-and-services-payments-help277); [Cautellus "Skip the Fee" scam explainer](https://cautellus.com/blog/paypal-goods-services-vs-friends-family-scam) (search summary)
- MELON (agency, ~2021) paid contractors by **bank ACH, PayPal, or with "payment conversion assistance for international team members"** — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/now-hiring-experienced-scripters-builders-animators-modelers-ui-designers-for-full-time-work-usd-melon/1000407) (search summary)
- Payment terms on Discord listings vary: "**Upon Completion**", "**Partial Upfront**", "**Per Task**". Pay may be USD, Robux, gift cards or other forms — [RoHire](https://rohire.dev/) (search summary)
- Robux is "the dominant payment method in the Roblox commission market", especially for smaller commissions, while some clients think in USD, pay more and offer longer-term work — [RoPage.gg 2026 guide](https://ropage.gg/blog/how-to-get-your-first-roblox-commissions) (search summary)

**Scams and protections**
- Commission scams run both ways: the client disappears with the product, or the developer disappears with the payment. For scripters the advice is to **record a video of the working result** and **require payment before sending the script or .rblx**. Some suggest that after about 30 days unpaid you can reuse or sell the work — [DevForum "How to NOT get scammed while doing a commission" (~2021)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-not-get-scammed-while-doing-a-commission/1406522) (search summary). Follow-up thread: [DevForum "What to do after getting scammed for a commission" (~2024)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-to-do-after-getting-scammed-for-a-commission/3309705)
- 2025–26 scam patterns:
  - fake "moderators" who get developers to "verify" on fake Discord servers, impersonating real communities;
  - **phishing copies of the login page** that defeat even 2FA;
  - requests to **paste code into browser dev tools**, which steals session cookies;
  - convincing-looking accounts that ask you to **transfer assets or groups first, with payment promised within 14 days**, and never pay;
  - compromised groups having **all group funds paid out**.
  
  Roblox Support can restore Robux and groups through a **one-time rollback**. Sources: [DevForum "Most common scams you should be aware of" (~2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/most-common-scams-you-should-be-aware-of/4642306); [DevForum "I was scammed by two fake developers after a game transfer" (~2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-was-scammed-by-two-fake-developers-after-a-game-transfer-%E2%80%93-a-warning-to-others/3859583); [DevForum "Roblox Account Hacked: Lost group with 2 Million Robux" (~2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-account-hacked-lost-group-with-2-million-robux/3448078); [DevForum "How i was scammed out of my group and 5 million robux" (~2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-i-was-scammed-out-of-my-group-and-5-million-robux/4724698); [DevForum "Group and Development Assets Stolen" (~2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/group-and-development-assets-stolen/4059849) (search summary)
- PayPal chargebacks are decided by the issuing bank, not PayPal, and PayPal may not contest them effectively. These are weak, non-Roblox community sources — [eBay community thread](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Charge-Back-scammers/td-p/30764953) (search summary)

### Inferences
- **Price in USD, or set the Robux price using the DevEx rate.** $1 is about 263 R$ at $0.0038. A Robux-paying client therefore effectively spends about 3.3 times your DevEx value: about $9.9k of Robux purchase value to deliver $3k to you. That explains why Robux-only clients rarely pay expert rates.
- If you accept Robux: the 2026 change to pending payouts means delays of 3 days to a month or more. Payouts to a payer from a compromised or rolled-back group could in principle be reversed; I found no direct confirmation of clawbacks.
- **Goods & Services protects the client, not you.** A client can open a dispute after delivery. Protection comes from milestone escrow (Upwork/Fiverr), deposits, or keeping code or place files until each milestone is paid, plus video proof.
- **Never** transfer groups or assets, run dev-tools code, or "verify" on a third-party server for an unknown client. These match the current documented scam patterns.

### Gaps
- No Roblox-community evidence was found on **Wise, crypto (for example USDT) or third-party escrow** for commissions, or on how often they are used. PayPal's availability in different countries was not checked.
- Whether PayPal **Seller Protection covers intangible services** such as code was not checked.
- The **game-pass route** (client buys a pass from you) and Roblox's marketplace cut on game-pass sales could not be checked before the search budget ran out.
- No sources were found on **"free test task" scams**, stolen-asset traps, or **Robux paid from stolen accounts being clawed back** from recipients.
- No sources were found on contract or NDA templates or norms for Roblox commissions, or on deposit percentages (for example 30% vs 50% upfront).
- It is unclear whether Robux received via group payouts can qualify for the new US 18+ DevEx rate of $0.0054.

  - At the Discord listing averages ($134–$219), **≈ 14–22 average-size jobs/month**, or 3–5 per week. That is feasible for a fast full-timer on small tasks, but depends on steady deal flow and on scope creep staying under control.
  - At Upwork's typical $15–$35/hr, **86–200 billed hours/month** before fees. At a senior $50/hr, about 60 billed hours.
- **The most realistic path to $3k+:**
  - one or two **USD retainers or contracts** (for example $1.5k–$3k/month each) with studios, agencies or games that already earn money;
  - plus **mid-size system commissions** ($500–$1,500 each) found through HiddenDevs, DevForum, RoHire and Talent Hub;
  - avoid percentage-only deals unless there is upfront pay or proof of existing revenue.
- Full-time studio offers from 2020–21 ($3.5k–$5k/month, $48k–$84k/yr) show that $3k+/month has been achievable for strong scripters on long-term contracts. Whether those offers are as plentiful in 2025–26 could not be confirmed.
