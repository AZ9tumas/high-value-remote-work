## 1. Creator Store paid assets: how selling plugins and models works in 2025–2026, history of Robux plugins, what sells, and any earnings data

### Takeaway
Since 2024 for plugins and since 10 Dec 2025 for models, Roblox lets ID-verified individual creators sell plugins and models on the Creator Store, in USD only. Payouts go through Stripe, and the seller keeps "100% of net proceeds" after taxes and payment processing. Price bands are $4.99–$249.99 for plugins and $2.99–$49.99 for models. There is a 30-day escrow, and payouts happen once a month on the 1st. Roblox has published no absolute sales figures. The only official performance number is relative: "2.5X more net income" than the old Robux model (stated at RDC 2024). Rules ban obfuscated code and remote requires. As a result, code-only products cannot be technically protected after purchase. Only Restricted meshes and images inside a paid model get access protection.

### Cited Findings
**Eligibility and onboarding (official docs, read 2026-10-05)**
- To distribute assets at all, the account must be at least 2 days old, not recently banned, and verified by an age check or government ID. "You cannot verify with a phone number." — [Roblox creator-docs: creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- To sell, you also need a Stripe seller account and 2-Step Verification on the Roblox account. You must be 18+, or 13–17 with a parent/guardian who agrees to the terms and is entered on the Stripe form. You must live in a country Stripe's cross-border payouts support. "Seller onboarding is not yet supported in countries such as Brazil, China, India, or Russia." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Stripe onboarding collects tax IDs (TIN) where applicable and a W-8/W-9 form. Each Roblox account gets one Stripe account. Once the account is approved, its country cannot be changed. Editing details after approval puts the account back to "Pending" and freezes payouts. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- "You can only sell assets that you own from an **individual** user account. Group-owned assets are ineligible." Groups also cannot *buy* USD-priced models or plugins: "Only individual user accounts may purchase assets for USD." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Distribution limits per 30 days: verified accounts can distribute 200 models and 10 plugins; unverified accounts 10 models and 2 plugins. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)

**Pricing, revenue share and payouts**
- "Plugins have a minimum price of $4.99 and maximum price of $249.99." "Models have a minimum price of $2.99 and maximum price of $49.99." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md); the minimums are repeated on [creator-docs: monetization/index.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md)
- Roblox's wording: "Sell a Model or Plugin and earn 100% of net proceeds on transactions… only taxes and payment processing fees are deducted", which bypasses "platform fees and DevEx rates." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Conflicting phrasing: one search summary of the Creator Store announcement threads says "after Roblox's 10% tax and fee deduction, creators will be paid 90% of all transactions." The exact page could not be confirmed. — (via search summary) [DevForum: Buy and Sell Models on the Creator Store](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/buy-and-sell-models-on-the-creator-store/4142112). Another summary paraphrases Roblox's monetization page as "creators earn up to 90% for things they sell in real money (such as plugins…)" — (via search summary) [Roblox: Turn your creativity into income](https://create.roblox.com/docs/monetize)
- USD only: "the real-world pricing model does not support Robux. You can only price assets on the Creator Store in USD." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Payout timing: each sale is held in "a 30 day escrow period," then "earnings automatically pay out once a month… on the first of every month." Stripe's minimum payout amounts apply in some currencies. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Refunds and chargebacks are deducted from the seller. If a very high share of an asset's sales comes from accounts with a history of chargebacks, the asset is taken off sale and payouts are frozen. If an asset is moderated after a sale, a reimbursement is deducted unless an appeal succeeds within 5 days. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)

**Content rules that matter for code products**
- Banned practices: "Obscuring engine features within scripts" (LuaVMs, getfenv/setfenv); "Requiring remote assets" (`require(assetId)`, `loadstring()`, `InsertService:LoadAsset()`, `AssetService:LoadAssetAsync()`, `LinkedSource`); "Including obfuscated code"; "Extremely large scripts." — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Anti-copy protection covers only Restricted child assets. If someone forks a game that contains a paid model, "none of the model's Restricted child image and mesh assets will load unless that person has also purchased the model." Roblox recommends turning on Asset Privacy so images, meshes and decals are Restricted by default. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)
- Additional rules for paid models (announced 10 Dec 2025): fewer than 15,000 dependencies; no Restricted images or meshes created by others; any referenced audio or video must itself be on the Creator Store. — (via search summary) [DevForum: Buy and Sell Models on the Creator Store](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/buy-and-sell-models-on-the-creator-store/4142112)

### Inferences
- **Revenue math (my estimate):** if taxes plus processing take about 5–10%, reaching **$3,000/month net** from one product needs roughly 316–334 sales/month at $9.99, 211–222 at $14.99, 105–111 at $29.99, or 63–67 at $49.99. $49.99 is the cap for models; plugins can go up to $249.99. That volume is plausible only for a top-tier utility. A realistic first-year planning range for a new seller with one or two good products is **about $50–$500/month**. A breakout plugin might reach the low thousands, but Roblox has published no absolute figures to confirm this.
- **Time to first revenue:** build time (about 2–6 weeks for a polished plugin or system), plus Stripe onboarding, plus the 30-day escrow, plus the payout on the 1st. That puts the first bank deposit about **1.5–3 months** after starting.
- **Requirements fit:** a highly experienced scripter in a Stripe-supported country qualifies easily: ID verification, 2SV, an individual account and Stripe. Roblox names Brazil, China, India and Russia as examples of unsupported countries; the list is not exhaustive. Scripters in unsupported countries cannot sell at all and must use off-platform stores (Section 2).
- **Code products cannot be protected.** Obfuscation and remote loaders are banned, and access protection only covers Restricted meshes and images. A code-only model can therefore be read and redistributed by any buyer. Plugins and models that bundle proprietary meshes, UI images or service-backed features, or that receive frequent updates, are easier to defend than pure Luau modules.
- The model-selling cap of $49.99 suggests Roblox sees models as mid-priced kits. Plugins, with a cap of $249.99, are the better fit for premium developer tools.

### Gaps
- No absolute Creator Store sales or revenue figures (total paid out, number of sellers, top-seller earnings) were found. Creator Store Analytics shows top sellers only to their own creators.
- The exact date of the USD plugin launch (believed to be around spring 2024) and the full text of DevForum 2923445 and 4142112 could not be fetched (devforum.roblox.com was blocked).
- The "90% after a 10% deduction" phrasing vs "100% of net proceeds" could not be reconciled from a primary page. The two are probably consistent if taxes plus processing come to about 10%, but this is unverified.
- No public earnings figures from individual paid-plugin sellers (for example, the authors of Moon Animator 2 or DataStore Editor) were found.

## 2. Off-platform marketplaces for Roblox systems and kits (BuiltByBit, Gumroad, Payhip, Ko-fi, Patreon, itch.io): fees, volume, what sells, licensing, anti-leak

### Takeaway
Fees are known for most of these platforms; Payhip and itch.io were not verified. BuiltByBit charges a 9.9% platform fee plus a reduced gateway fee and pays out through a Tebex Wallet after about 3 days. Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50, or 30% on sales through its Discover marketplace. Ko-fi charges 5% on its free plan and 0% with Gold at $12/month. Patreon charges new creators 10% since 4 Aug 2025. No sales-volume data for Roblox kits on any of these platforms could be verified. A 2026 comparison names KW Studio, ClearlyDev and BuiltByBit as the "Big 3" Roblox asset marketplaces. The main advantages over the Creator Store are faster payouts, availability in countries Stripe doesn't support, and higher price ceilings. The main risk is leaks.

### Cited Findings
- **BuiltByBit:** "9.9% platform fee on each transaction," plus "a reduced gateway fee" and possible currency-conversion fees when withdrawing in a currency other than USD. Minimum prices range from "$1.99 to $5.99… depending on category, with no maximum." Payouts go through a partnership with **Tebex** into a Tebex Wallet; "after a brief settlement period (usually 3 days)," creators withdraw via PayPal or bank. — (via search summary) [BuiltByBit: Become a creator](https://builtbybit.com/resources/creators); secondary: [HytaleModding docs on BuiltByBit](https://github.com/HytaleModding/site/blob/main/content/docs/en/publishing/builtbybit.mdx)
- **The "Big 3" Roblox asset marketplaces:** a 2026 article titled "KW Studio vs ClearlyDev vs BuiltByBit: Comparing the Big 3 Roblox Asset Marketplaces in 2026" is hosted on a Payhip store called "marketplaceroblox." It may have a conflict of interest, and its content could not be fetched. — [Payhip blog](https://payhip.com/marketplaceroblox/blog/news/kw-studio-vs-clearlydev-vs-builtbybit)
- **Gumroad:** "On January 31, 2023, Gumroad flattened everything to a single 10% effective rate." It charges "10% + $0.50 on direct sales… and a flat 30% on sales through its Discover marketplace." — (via search summary) [ecomm.design Gumroad review](https://ecomm.design/gumroad-review/); [GumroadAlternatives fee comparison](https://gumroadalternatives.com/alternatives)
- **Ko-fi:** the free plan charges "a 5% service fee on shop sales, memberships, and commissions"; "Ko-fi Gold costs $12/month and eliminates the 5% service fee." — (via search summary) [CreatorStackClub Ko-fi Shop review 2026](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/ko-fi-shop); [Ecommerce-Platforms: Ko-fi alternatives 2026](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/best-ko-fi-alternatives)
- **Patreon:** creators launching a new page from **4 Aug 2025** go on a "Standard plan with a 10% fee," which replaces the old Pro (8%) and Premium (12%) tiers. Existing creators keep their rates. Payment processing (about 2.9% + $0.30) and currency-conversion fees (about 2.5%) come on top, so realistic take-home is "around 80–85% of gross pledges." — (via search summary) [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/patreon-increases-its-prices-for-new-creators); [Digital Music News, 2025-06-18](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/06/18/patreon-ups-the-cut-it-takes-from-creators-to-10/); [Patron.com blog](https://www.patron.com/blog/post/patreon-fee-changes-2025/)
- **itch.io:** it has a live "Top selling paid tools tagged roblox-studio-plugin" listing, which shows paid Roblox plugins are sold there. Its contents and fee terms could not be fetched. — [itch.io tag page](https://itch.io/tools/top-sellers/store/tag-roblox-studio-plugin)
- **Roblox-native donation model used by open-source projects:** the Adonis admin system's FUNDING.yml links Ko-fi plus Roblox game passes ("Adonis Donor Perks," "50/100/500 No Perks"). The sift library links a Roblox group store. — [Epix-Incorporated/Adonis repo (.github/FUNDING.yml)](https://github.com/Epix-Incorporated/Adonis); [csqrl/sift repo (.github/FUNDING.yml)](https://github.com/csqrl/sift)
- **Contrast with Creator Store rules:** on the Creator Store, obfuscation and remote `require`/`loadstring` loaders are banned, and access control only covers Restricted meshes and images. — [creator-store.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)

### Inferences
- **Fees per $20 sale (my calculation, before payment gateway or VAT where not stated):** BuiltByBit keeps about $18.02 for the seller (9.9%) plus a gateway fee. Gumroad direct keeps $17.50 (10% + $0.50). Gumroad Discover keeps $14.00. Ko-fi free keeps $19.00 before PayPal/Stripe fees. Payhip and itch.io: not verified (see Gaps).
- **Who these suit:** sellers in countries Stripe doesn't support (Brazil, India and others), sellers who want faster settlement (BuiltByBit's about 3 days vs the Creator Store's 30-day escrow plus monthly payout), and sellers of products priced above the Creator Store's $49.99 model cap, such as full game kits or frameworks. Off-platform delivery is a downloaded .rbxm/.rbxl or a Git repo, which leaks even more easily than a Creator Store model.
- **Earning potential (estimate, unverified by any sales data):** without an existing audience, expect **about $0–$300/month** in the first six months. With a Discord or YouTube funnel and a flagship kit (combat framework, data and inventory system, admin panel, UI kit), **$500–$1,500/month** is a plausible stretch. Reaching $3,000+/month from kits alone would need a known brand.
- **Anti-leak practice (inference; no source found):** the realistic defenses are support, updates, documentation and Discord access tied to the buyer; licenses tied to the buyer's identity; and keeping valuable logic in services the seller hosts. Remote code loaders are banned on the Creator Store, and off-platform code that phones home raises trust and Roblox-ToS concerns with buyers.
- **Time to first revenue:** about 2–6 weeks to build and list a kit. Payout takes about 3 days on BuiltByBit and is near-immediate to weekly on the other stores (not verified per platform).

### Gaps
- No sales-volume, revenue or top-seller data was found for any Roblox kit on BuiltByBit, Gumroad, Payhip, Ko-fi, KW Studio or ClearlyDev.
- Fees for Payhip, itch.io's default revenue share, KW Studio and ClearlyDev were not retrieved: the sites were blocked and the search budget was used up.
- No documented BuiltByBit leak-protection or DMCA process for Roblox assets was found.
- No source was found on how Roblox's Terms of Use treat paid off-platform scripts or licensing schemes. Avoid recommending remote-loader DRM without verifying that it is allowed.

## 3. Open source plus sponsorships: do maintainers of popular Roblox libraries earn meaningful money, and does open source lead to paid work?

### Takeaway
No. Live GitHub Sponsors pages for well-known Luau maintainers show 1–5 current sponsors each, mostly at $5/month tiers. Most flagship Roblox libraries don't even publish a FUNDING.yml. Open source is worth doing as a portfolio and reputation signal for jobs and contracts, but not as an income stream. One concrete example of a career link is Knit's author, a software engineer whose GitHub profile lists the "@easy-games" organization. I take this to be Easy.gg, the studio behind BedWars, but that identification is mine and was not verified here.

### Cited Findings (GitHub, live as of 2026-10-05)
- **Fusion (dphfox):** 797 stars; **1 current sponsor, 27 past sponsors**; a "$5 a month" tier (name in release notes) or a custom amount. — [GitHub Sponsors: dphfox](https://github.com/sponsors/dphfox). Profile status: "Building something neat." — [github.com/dphfox](https://github.com/dphfox)
- **roblox-ts (osyrisrblx):** **5 current sponsors**, goal of 20 sponsors, "25% towards goal"; "Donations would enable me to spend more time working on roblox-ts." — [GitHub Sponsors: osyrisrblx](https://github.com/sponsors/osyrisrblx)
- **selene, full-moon, DataStore2 (Kampfkarren):** **2 current sponsors**, $5/month tier; selene has 826 stars and full-moon 539. Bio: "i do not do much open source stuff anymore… i'm in the process of making my projects unnecessary." — [GitHub Sponsors: Kampfkarren](https://github.com/sponsors/Kampfkarren); [github.com/Kampfkarren](https://github.com/Kampfkarren)
- **Janitor (howmanysmall):** 149 stars; **1 current, 3 past sponsors**; goal of $20/month at "0% progress." — [GitHub Sponsors: howmanysmall](https://github.com/sponsors/howmanysmall)
- **Knit (Sleitnick):** Knit (630 stars) and RbxUtil (466 stars) fund through Ko-fi (`ko_fi: sleitnick` in FUNDING.yml); the Ko-fi totals aren't public. His profile says "Software engineer," Ohio, affiliated with **@easy-games**. — [Sleitnick/Knit repo](https://github.com/Sleitnick/Knit); [github.com/Sleitnick](https://github.com/Sleitnick)
- **evaera (Cmdr 530 stars, Promise 350, moonwave 247, matter 142):** Promise's FUNDING.yml points to Patreon "erynlynn." The profile lists no employer. — [evaera/roblox-lua-promise](https://github.com/evaera/roblox-lua-promise); [github.com/evaera](https://github.com/evaera)
- **Other funding links:** NevermoreEngine → Patreon "quenty" ([repo](https://github.com/Quenty/NevermoreEngine)); pesde → Ko-fi and GitHub "daimond113" ([repo](https://github.com/pesde-pkg/pesde)); Adonis → Ko-fi plus Roblox donation game passes ([repo](https://github.com/Epix-Incorporated/Adonis)).
- **No `.github/FUNDING.yml` found** (main and master branches checked) for MadStudioRoblox/ProfileStore, MadStudioRoblox/ProfileService, SirMallard/Iris, evaera/Cmdr, 1ForeverHD/TopbarPlus, jsdotlua/react-lua, Ukendio/jecs, rojo-rbx/rojo, UpliftGames/wally, or JohnnyMorganz's StyLua and luau-lsp. JohnnyMorganz also has no GitHub Sponsors page. — checked via raw.githubusercontent.com on 2026-10-05; e.g. [ProfileStore repo](https://github.com/MadStudioRoblox/ProfileStore), [TopbarPlus repo](https://github.com/1ForeverHD/TopbarPlus), [github.com/JohnnyMorganz](https://github.com/JohnnyMorganz)

### Inferences
- **Expected sponsorship income:** about **$0–$50/month**, even for a widely used library. The best-known Luau toolchain project in this sample (roblox-ts) had 5 sponsors. Treat open source as marketing, not revenue.
- **Indirect value (inference):** open source is a credible portfolio for studio jobs and high-rate contracts. Sleitnick's Easy.gg affiliation fits that pattern, though causation isn't documented. It also works as the free base of an **open-core** model: a free library plus a paid premium plugin, kit, support tier or course. That is likely the best way to turn open-source reputation into money.
- **Time to first revenue:** only indirect, and typically months to years. Sponsorship alone shouldn't be counted toward the $3,000/month goal.

### Gaps
- Ko-fi and Patreon earnings for Sleitnick, evaera and Quenty couldn't be checked (ko-fi.com and patreon.com were blocked; those totals are often hidden anyway).
- No primary source (interview or post) was found in which a maintainer credits open source for getting hired or contracted. Whether loleris (ProfileStore), ForeverHD (TopbarPlus) or the Iris maintainers earn indirectly wasn't researched.
- Star counts come from GitHub pages fetched 2026-10-05. The GitHub API was blocked, so last-commit dates weren't checked.

## 4. Content and education: YouTube tutorial income, paid courses, 1:1 tutoring, and kids' coding-instructor jobs

### Takeaway
Large Roblox scripting channels exist: TheDevKing has about 665K subscribers and AlvinBlox about 453K. Social Blade estimates AlvinBlox's ad income at a very wide $281–$4.5K/month, so YouTube is slow to start and uncertain. Paid Roblox scripting courses are priced around $50–$100 (Crusherfire Media's early-access price was $49.95). Kids' coding-instructor jobs are the fastest and most reliable income here, but pay is low: aggregators report an average of about $20–$22/hour for online Roblox instructors and tutors in mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
**YouTube channels (Social Blade data via search summary, snapshot about Oct 2026, exact date unknown)**
- **TheDevKing:** "665K subscribers, 93,943,264 views, and 169 videos." — (via search summary) [Social Blade: TheDevKing](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/thedevking)
- **AlvinBlox:** "453K subscribers… 51,140,072 total video views… estimated monthly earnings of $281 – $4.5K and yearly earnings of $3.4K – $54K." — (via search summary) [Social Blade: AlvinBlox](https://socialblade.com/youtube/c/alvinblox)
- **BrawlDev:** the channel exists ([YouTube @BrawlDevRBLX](https://www.youtube.com/@BrawlDevRBLX)). A search found "no specific pricing information for a formal 'BrawlDev Academy' or paid scripting course." — (via search summary)
- **Roblox's own recognition of video creators:** the Innovation Awards 2025 include a "Best Video Star" category (winner: WaffleTrades). — (via search summary) [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2025-innovation-awards-winners/)

## 6. Security: Roblox's HackerOne bug bounty (scope, payouts, whether engine/Luau bugs qualify) and legitimate anti-exploit consulting

### Takeaway
Roblox runs a public HackerOne program. Maximum payouts are $1–$300 for Low, $300–$1,000 for Medium, $1,000–$5,000 for High and $5,000–$20,000 for Critical, plus bonuses. Average payouts are $675 for Medium, $2,875 for High and $11,100 for Critical. Engine and Luau bugs do qualify. Luau's own security policy treats any memory-safety violation as a vulnerability and sends reports to Roblox's HackerOne. In late 2024 Roblox ran a 1.5× promotion just for engine bugs. These finds need C++/VM security skills (fuzzing, memory corruption) beyond gameplay scripting, so income is lumpy and unpredictable. No pricing data was found for anti-exploit consulting.

### Cited Findings
- **Payout ranges:** the "maximum amount payable for a qualifying report" is "$1–$300 for Low, $300–$1,000 for Medium, $1,000–$5,000 for High, and $5,000–$20,000 for Critical, not including bonuses." — (via search summary; aggregator) [hacking.community: Roblox program](https://hacking.community/programs/roblox); program page [HackerOne: Roblox](https://hackerone.com/roblox)
- **Average bounties:** "Medium - $675 (43.74% of submissions), High - $2,875 (21.37%), Critical - $11,100 (6.87%)." Low makes up about 28% of submissions. — (via search summary) [HackerOne: Roblox](https://hackerone.com/roblox); [hacking.community](https://hacking.community/programs/roblox)
- **Bonuses:** "Up to $200 bonus for well documented reports with clear reproducible steps and exploit code," and "up to $500 bonus for validating complicated fixes." — (via search summary) [HackerOne: Roblox](https://hackerone.com/roblox)
- **Scope notes:** there are 7 in-scope assets. Out of scope: missing HTTP headers that don't lead directly to a vulnerability; volumetric DDoS/DoS/spam. However, "vulnerabilities in the Roblox data model for crashing game servers are encouraged to be reported." Findings on `*.ra.roblox.com` that don't affect release servers are out of scope. — (via search summary) [HackerOne: Roblox](https://hackerone.com/roblox)
- **Engine-focused promotion** [OLDER, 2024]: from 16 Oct to 6 Nov 2024, valid engine reports paid "1.5 times the standard program amount." Priority went to denial of service and memory-safety bugs (use-after-free, out-of-bounds read/write, double free, uninitialized memory reads). — (via search summary) [DevForum: Bug Bounty Event – Engine Security](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/bug-bounty-event-engine-security/3190899)
- **Luau is explicitly covered:** "Luau provides a safe sandbox that scripts can not escape from… This includes the virtual machine, builtin libraries and native code generation facilities. Any source code can not result in memory safety errors or crashes during its compilation or execution. Violations of memory safety are considered vulnerabilities… You can report security bugs via [HackerOne](https://hackerone.com/roblox)." The same policy says feeding non-compiler bytecode is unsupported, with no security guarantees. — [luau-lang/luau SECURITY.md](https://github.com/luau-lang/luau/blob/master/SECURITY.md)
- Roblox's help center explains how to report security exploits. — [Roblox Help: How to Report Security Exploits](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038516512-How-to-Report-Security-Exploits)
- **Anti-exploit know-how is commoditized** in free 2026 guides on RemoteEvent security ("never trust the client"). — [gmmarket.me: Roblox RemoteEvent Security (2026 Guide)](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/roblox-remoteevent-security-how-exploits-work-and-how-to-stop-them-never-trust-t)

### Inferences
- **Fit for a Luau scripter:** most high-value Roblox bounties seem to need native-code skills: fuzzing the Luau VM, compiler or codegen, or finding engine memory bugs and server-crash data-model bugs. An expert in server-authoritative design may find **data-model server-crash bugs** (explicitly encouraged) and logic or replication flaws. Expected value is very uneven: maybe $0 for many months, then $675–$11,100 for a single valid finding at average Medium-to-Critical payouts. It shouldn't count toward a reliable $3,000/month.
- **Time to first revenue:** unknown, typically months. HackerOne triage and payout times weren't found.
- **Anti-exploit consulting or audits (inference):** this is legitimate and ToS-compliant when framed as a defensive review: auditing RemoteEvents and RemoteFunctions, server-side validation, rate limiting, DataStore integrity and economy exploits. It is best sold as a fixed-price audit add-on to commission work, for example as a productized "remote security audit" for a mid-size game. The pricing is a guess and needs verification. Do not build or sell executors, bypasses or client-side "anti-cheat" that relies on obfuscation (also banned on the Creator Store).

### Gaps
- The HackerOne program page couldn't be fetched directly. The payout ranges come from a third-party aggregator plus search snippets, and the current 2026 table may differ. Total bounties paid and response times weren't retrieved.
- Whether Studio and plugin-API bugs, or Open Cloud API bugs, are among the 7 in-scope assets wasn't confirmed.
- No market data (rates, demand, examples) for paid Roblox anti-exploit audits or consulting was found.

## 8. UGC avatar items: economics in 2025–2026 and whether they suit a scripter

### Takeaway
UGC means avatar items such as accessories, clothing and bodies. It is a 3D-art business: creators get 30% of Marketplace sales, rising to as much as 70% when priced well above the floor. In-game sales pay the creator 30% and the game owner 40%. Eligibility needs ID verification plus Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200, and creators pay upload fees and refundable publishing advances of up to 20,000 Robux. Earnings are paid in Robux and converted through DevEx at $0.0038. Third-party claims of a "$330M" UGC market can't be verified. UGC is a poor direct fit for a scripter except through a partnership with a modeler, or by building the in-game shops and try-on systems that earn the 40% game-owner commission.

### Cited Findings
- **Eligibility:** "Creators who are ID-verified, have Roblox Plus or Roblox Premium 1000 or 2200, and consistently follow our Community Standards" can sell. The legacy UGC Program application is being sunset. — [creator-docs: marketplace FAQ](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions.md)
- **Fees:** 80 Robux per upload (500 Robux for items with emissive masks). Refundable publishing advance, for example: hats and faces 1,500 Robux (13,000 for paid Limited); hair 1,000 (6,000); bodies 2,500 (20,000); 3D clothing such as jackets, pants and shirts 600 (5,000); classic clothing 10. Emissive items pay five times the standard non-limited advance. — [creator-docs: marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md)
- **Revenue split:** Marketplace purchases pay the creator 30% and Roblox 70%. A progressive share for pricing above the floor gives 37% at 1.3×, 50% at 2×, 62% at 3× and a maximum of 70% at 6× or more. In-game purchases pay the creator 30%, the game owner 40% and Roblox 30%. — [marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md)
- **Limiteds:** on resale, the reseller gets 50%, the original creator 10%, the seller or affiliate 10% and Roblox 30%. Commissions are held for 30 days. Free Limiteds cost a per-unit fee ("As of today… 100 Robux"). Creators can publish only one Limited per day. — [fees doc](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md); [marketplace FAQ](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions.md)
- **Cash conversion:** DevEx pays $0.0038 per Earned Robux (since 5 Sep 2025), and cashing out needs at least 30,000 Robux. — [developer-exchange.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/developer-exchange.md)
- **Market-size claims (unverified; likely AI-written sites):** "The UGC fashion market generated $330 million in 2025"; "the median UGC creator earns less than 5,000 Robux per month"; "estimated 120,000 active sellers as of early 2026"; "median UGC item in 2025 earned roughly $20 lifetime." The search summary didn't say which page each figure came from, and none cites Roblox primary data. — (via search summary) [rolearn.dev UGC trends 2026](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/roblox-ugc-marketplace-trends-2026/); [RoWatcher: Inside Roblox's UGC economy 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/inside-roblox-s-ugc-economy-who-s-winning-in-2026); [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-creator-economy)
- **Recognition:** Best UGC Creator 2025 went to Yourius, and Best Original UGC to "Knitted Arm Warmers Very White." — (via search summary) [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2025-innovation-awards-winners/)

### Inferences
- **Revenue math:** $3,000/month through DevEx is about 789,474 Earned Robux/month. At the base 30% creator share, that means about 2.63M Robux of gross Marketplace sales per month. UGC can't be a scripter's main secondary stream without a modeling partner and a hit catalog.
- **Better angle for a scripter:** build avatar shops, try-on and outfit systems, and catalog integrations for game owners as commission or productized work. Game owners earn 40% on in-game UGC sales, so a well-built shop is a sellable Creator Store model or kit. Another option is a revenue-share partnership with a 3D modeler, where the scripter handles marketing and analytics, but the scripter's share there would be small.
- **Time to first revenue:** after eligibility (ID plus Plus or Premium) and up-front Robux costs (upload fees and advances), a first sale is possible within weeks. Cash requires reaching DevEx's 30,000-Robux minimum plus the 30-day escrow. Planning figure for a scripter without a modeler: about $0–$100/month.

- **Overall inference:** for a highly experienced scripter, the most realistic diversified path to $3,000+/month **from secondary streams** combines three things. First, **predictable hourly work** (independent tutoring or an instructor job) as a $1–2k floor. Second, **one or two premium products** (a Creator Store plugin, plus a higher-priced kit off-platform), growing toward $500–$1.5k. Third, **B2B tooling or audit retainers** for a handful of games. Content (YouTube) is the long-run funnel that compounds product sales. Sponsorships, awards, jams, bounties and UGC are upside or reputation plays rather than plan-able income. All of these ranges are estimates; none of the primary sources disclosed per-seller sales.
