> **How this was researched (read first):** I tried WebFetch on 11 hosts (careers.play.vc, builtin.com, gamefam.com, uplift.games, tubefilter.com, digiday.com, prnewswire.com, globenewswire.com, hitmarker.net, reviewjournal.com and kore1.com). The session's egress policy blocked every one. All findings below therefore come from search-engine excerpts of the cited URLs, not from full-page reads. Pay figures and dates are given as the excerpts stated them. Many job-post dates could not be confirmed, so those posts are marked "date unverified". Spot-check the key numbers before publishing. Research date: 5 Oct 2026.

## 1. Which studios and companies hire Roblox developers in 2025–2026? Listing examples, remote policy and international eligibility

### Takeaway
Many Roblox-native studios hire Luau engineers. The best-paid salaried roles, however, are mostly limited to US residents (Gamefam) or US/UK residents (Uplift Games). For candidates outside the US and UK, the realistic routes are:
- remote-first studios that say they hire worldwide (Paradoxum Games, The Gang, Toya);
- Twin Atlas's worldwide contractor pool, paid monthly in USD;
- full-time hourly contracts with no fixed end date (for example Creator Games at US$30–40/hr).

### Inferences
- For a candidate outside the US and UK, the large headline employers shrink to a handful:
  - Paradoxum, The Gang and Toya, which say they hire globally or use remote freelancers;
  - Twin Atlas's contractor pool;
  - hourly "full-time contract" roles such as Creator Games.
- Gamefam and Uplift, the most visible pay-transparent studios, exclude non-US or non-US/UK applicants for salaried roles.
- Studios that own hit IP (Uplift, Paradoxum, Twin Atlas, BIG Games) look like more stable employers than brand-activation agencies (Super League/Supersocial, MELON, Dubit, The Gang's agency side), which depend on brand budgets (see Q6).
- Several top game groups (BIG Games, many DevForum studios) do not run public job boards. Visibility in the community (DevForum, Talent Hub creator pages, X/Discord) is therefore a hiring channel in itself, not just applications.

### Gaps
- I found no specific 2025–26 job listings or company data for **Wonderstruck, Fluid, "Beyond", Metaverse Team, Gamer Robot** or "Dapper Labs-style" brand studios. Searches returned only generic job-board pages. They may be inactive, renamed, or hire privately.
- Whether Gamefam is hiring Luau programmers in 2026 is unknown. The pay-transparent listing found appears to be from 2024–25.
- The exact posting dates of most listings (Uplift, Paradoxum, Twin Atlas DevForum, Creator Games, Toya) could not be verified because page fetches were blocked.
- Whether the Roblox Talent Hub is still operating in 2026, or has been replaced, is unconfirmed. The DevForum "Find and Hire Talent" category clearly still carries hiring posts.

## 2. Compensation data: salary ranges, hourly contract rates and how international contractors are paid

### Takeaway
Pay-transparent salaried studio roles cluster at **US$60K–$140K/yr** (about US$5K–$11.7K/month gross). Roblox Corporation's own Luau-heavy roles pay **US$150K–$458K**. International-friendly contracts are lower but still above the US$3,000/month target for an experienced scripter:
- Twin Atlas contractors: **US$3,000–4,000/month**;
- Creator Games: **US$30–40/hr full-time** (about US$5.2K–6.9K/month);
- freelance roles: **US$40–60/hr**.

DevForum and Talent Hub posts vary widely and are often far below market, especially when pay is in Robux, which converts at US$0.0038 per Robux through DevEx.

### Inferences
- **Monthly equivalents:**
  - US$60K/yr ≈ US$5,000/month; US$65K ≈ US$5,417/month; US$140K ≈ US$11,667/month.
  - US$30/hr × 40 h × 52 wk ÷ 12 ≈ **US$5,200/month**; US$40/hr ≈ **US$6,933/month**.
  - Roblox's US$20–25/hr contract role ≈ US$3,467–4,333/month.
- **Robux pay valued at the standard DevEx rate (US$0.0038):**
  - 1,000,000 R$ ≈ US$3,800;
  - 20,000 R$ ≈ US$76;
  - 1,800 R$ per task ≈ US$6.84.
  - Many 2025–26 DevForum offers are therefore far below professional rates. Robux pay only clears US$3K/month at around 800K R$/month or more, and only if the worker is DevEx-eligible.
- An experienced international scripter can most plausibly reach US$3K+/month through one of three routes:
  - a monthly-retainer contract (the Twin Atlas US$3–4K band sits right at the target);
  - a full-time hourly contract at US$25–40/hr;
  - a salaried role at a studio that hires internationally (e.g. Paradoxum's US$70–80K band, if available in the candidate's country).

1. a screen;
2. a 60–90 minute live technical interview with senior engineers;
3. team and leadership interviews.

They look for:

- professional engineering practice (Git/GitHub, Rojo; sometimes roblox-ts);

## 4. Revenue-share and equity-like arrangements: typical percentages, terms, payout mechanics, risks and how to evaluate offers

### Takeaway
2025–26 DevForum revenue-share offers to scripters are mostly from small, unfunded teams. They offer 3–10% individually, or a 40% pool shared across several roles, often alongside tiny Robux task fees. Older (2019) community norms suggested 45–75% for a permanent sole scripter on a complex game. Payouts flow through Roblox group recurring payouts, configurable per experience since a July 2024 beta, and are then converted via DevEx. Revenue share should be treated as upside on top of guaranteed pay: Roblox does not enforce private deals, and most games earn little.

Established studios use equity-like cash incentives instead. Uplift, for example, offers a 4-year vested "RSU-style" cash bonus plus an annual studio bonus.

### Inferences
- **Checklist for evaluating a revenue-share offer:**
  1. Is the game already live and earning? Ask for Creator Hub analytics or revenue screenshots.
  2. Is your split configured in-platform (per-experience recurring payout) rather than promised verbally?
  3. Is there a written agreement covering scope, vesting/cliff, what happens if you leave, IP ownership, and how the split survives a game sale or transfer? The Super League and Baobab deals show games can be sold.
  4. Is there a guaranteed base (USD retainer or hourly pay) alongside the percentage?
  5. What share does the owner keep, and how many other people share the pool?
- **Valuing a percentage:** a 10% share of a game earning 500K R$/month is 50K R$ ≈ US$190 at DevEx US$0.0038 (illustrative arithmetic). Reaching US$3K/month from 10% alone would need roughly 7.9M R$/month in group revenue, which puts the game in the top tier.
- Revenue share converted through DevEx adds friction (eligibility, cash-out timing, payout rails), which matters for international workers.

- I could not verify the current holding period ("pending Robux") before group revenue becomes available for payouts.
- I found no first-hand accounts (2025–26) of disputes or enforcement outcomes over revenue-share agreements.

## 6. Market conditions: is Roblox-studio hiring growing or shrinking in 2025–2026, and how competitive is it?

### Takeaway
The picture is mixed and cooling in 2026.
- **Growth through 2025:** creator payouts reached US$1.5B in 2025, Roblox grew headcount 24%, and brand activations rose to about 600.
- **Slowdown in 2026:** bookings growth fell to 8% in Q2 2026 and full-year guidance was reportedly cut sharply. Daily users and engagement hours are well below their Q3 2025 peaks.
- **Studio squeeze:** brand-service studios are consolidating and pivoting to owning game equity as Roblox's own ad sales compete for brand budgets.

Funding still flows to hit-making and Incubator studios. Remote roles are a minority and many are geography-restricted, so competition for international-friendly roles is likely high.

### Cited Findings

**Platform health**
- In FY2025, creators earned **US$1,503.1M** through DevEx, and over 35,500 creators were registered in DevEx at 31 Dec 2025. — [Roblox FY2025 Annual Report (ARS)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000110465926044380/rblx-20251231xars.pdf); [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- The top 1,000 creators reportedly averaged **US$1.1M** each in 2025, up 40%. This figure came from the search excerpt summarizing the same filings; it is not independently verified.
- **Q2 2026:**
  - Bookings were **US$1.6B, +8% year on year**, at the low end of guidance. Roblox blamed lower per-hour monetization among younger users in the US and Canada.

### Inferences
- **Demand is shifting from brand-service work to owned-IP and high-fidelity 18+ games.** The evidence:
  - brand-agency revenue is flat or squeezed (Super League);
  - Roblox competes for ad budgets;
  - Roblox is subsidizing 18+ high-fidelity games through Incubator/Jumpstart and the higher DevEx rate.
  - Studios with live hits (Uplift, Paradoxum, Twin Atlas, BIG Games) and newly funded studios (Versework, Incubator cohorts, Gamefam) are the likelier employers in late 2026.
- **The 2026 slowdown may tighten studio budgets.** Studio revenue depends on bookings, and bookings growth fell from 2025's pace to about 8%. This may favour contract and retainer hiring over full-time hiring, which could suit international contractors but means less job security.
- **Competition is likely rising.** Large games-industry layoffs (about 14K forecast for 2026) push experienced engine programmers toward Roblox roles. A Luau specialist's edge is proven live-game Roblox experience and tooling fluency.

### Gaps
- I found no hard 2025–26 data on studio-level layoffs or closures among Roblox-native studios (Gamefam, The Gang, MELON, Dubit, Twin Atlas, Uplift). Searches surfaced none, which is weak evidence of absence, not proof.
- I found no count of open Roblox-studio engineering roles over time (e.g. LinkedIn or Hitmarker trend data) to measure hiring growth directly.
- I could not confirm the KORE1 claim that guidance was cut from 22–26% to 8–12% against Roblox's own filings, because fetches were blocked.
