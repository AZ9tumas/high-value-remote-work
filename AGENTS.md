# Agent guide

Read this first. It applies to every agent in this repo (Claude, GPT Astra 6, others).

## Mission

Help the owner, a highly experienced, fast Roblox (Luau) scripter working full time, reach **at least US$3,000/month of legitimate online income**. Roblox first; other channels later.

## Start here

1. `board/PINNED.md`: goal, current phase, decisions, open questions.
2. `PLAN.md`: the action plan we are executing.
3. `reports/Roblox scripter income strategies.md`: the full research report, with sources. Read it with `reports/Codex review 2026-10-05.md`, the audit that corrected its tax steps and benchmarks.
4. `research_notes/Roblox scripter income strategies/`: raw notes per topic.
5. `PROFILE.md`: the owner's details. Tailor advice to it; ask on the board if something you need is missing.

## How we collaborate

- **Message board:** `board/` (protocol in `board/README.md`). On your first visit, post a short intro and add yourself to the roster.
- **Tasks:** `board/tasks/`. Claim before you start; mark done with a link to the result.
- **New research:** add dated, cited files under `research_notes/<topic>/`, then post a one-paragraph summary on the board.
- **Other agents' work:** don't rewrite it. Reply on the board, or add a clearly labelled section with your handle and date.

## Ground rules

1. **Legit only.** Nothing against Roblox ToS or the law: no exploits/executors, account or item selling, Robux reselling, gambling mechanics, or stolen/leaked assets.
2. **No outward actions without the owner's OK.** That covers contacting people, applying to jobs, posting publicly, creating accounts as the owner, and spending money.
3. **Cite and date.** Roblox rules and rates change often. Every number needs a source and the date it was checked.
4. **No secrets or personal data** in the repo.
5. **Be brief.** The owner prefers short explanations.

## Git

- Work on the repo's default branch (now `claude/charming-johnson-1poet5`, the first branch pushed), or open a PR into it.
- Pull before you write. Keep commits small and push promptly. Never force-push a shared branch.
- Commit prefixes: `board:`, `research:`, `plan:`, `tools:`.
