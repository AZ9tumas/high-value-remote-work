# Sell Systems for Dollars, Then Chase Hits

As of October 2026, the most reliable way for a highly experienced, fast Luau scripter to reach **US$3,000 a month** is to sell work for dollars rather than Robux, either through a long-term contract or salaried role with an established Roblox studio or through fixed-price system commissions and retainers for games that already make money. Own games are the only Roblox route with large upside, but at platform-average monetization a game needs about **330 sustained concurrent players** to earn that much, and the median DevEx participant earned roughly **$1,500 in the year to June 2026**. That gap sets the strategy: $36,000 a year is below every 2025–26 salaried studio band found yet about 24 times the median creator payout, so contracts form the floor and games are the upside. The skill set pays best where prices are fixed and output varies, which means per-system commissions, retainers and a pipeline of quick game prototypes that Roblox ads can test for under two cents a play. The plan runs in four stages: two weeks of setup and proof (including the **15 and 31 October 2026** DevEx tax dates), about eight weeks of dollar commissions alongside 8–12 studio applications, a contracted floor that delivers a first $3,000 month by about **January 2027** and is secured by month six, and a time-boxed game-and-product track from month four, with a month-nine checkpoint before any working hours move to own games. Creator Store plugins and similar products add hundreds of dollars a month, not thousands, and the fallbacks, in order, are FiveM Lua scripting, TypeScript and Discord-bot freelancing, and kids' coding instruction. Two caveats apply throughout: Roblox's quarterly creator payouts fell about **24%** from late 2025 to mid-2026, and the owner's profile (country, age, portfolio) is still blank, though it decides access to several studios and payout methods.

## $3,000 a month means 789,000 Earned Robux or one steady client

What the target demands depends on who pays. Through Roblox's Developer Exchange (DevEx), which pays the standard **$0.0038 per Earned Robux** on Robux earned since 5 September 2025, $3,000 equals **789,474 Earned Robux a month**. Creators can cash out at most once per calendar month, need at least 30,000 Robux to do so, and wait about 5–10 business days for processing ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/developer-exchange.md)). Creators keep 70% of the Robux price of passes and developer products ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus.md)), so a game must take in about 1.13 million Robux of player spending a month. The **$0.0054 US 18+ rate**, live since 8 June 2026, lowers that requirement by about 30%, but only for passes, developer products, subscriptions and private servers bought by age-verified US adults in games that use R15 avatars ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate.md)). When a client pays in dollars, the required rate is low and the difficulty is volume: a full-time hourly contract reaches $3,000 at about **$17.30 an hour**, and a freelancer who bills 60% of a full-time month needs about $29 an hour. The 60% is an assumption that leaves time for sales and admin, and $29 sits close to the $30 Upwork median for TypeScript work ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/typescript-developers/)).

| Route | What $3,000 a month requires | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time hourly contract | ≈$17.30/hr across ~173 hours | 40 hours × 52 weeks ÷ 12 |
| Freelance hours | ≈$29/hr across ~104 billable hours, or ~60 hours at $50/hr | Senior systems rates of $50–120+/hr ([Game-Ace](https://game-ace.com/blog/how-to-hire-the-best-roblox-game-developer/)) |
| Discord-sized commissions | 14–22 jobs | $134–219 average listing ([RoPage.gg](https://ropage.gg/blog/how-to-get-your-first-roblox-commissions); [RoHire](https://rohire.dev/sources/hidden-devs)) |
| Mid-size system commissions | 2–6 jobs | $500–1,500 jobs, within vendor price guides ([Memvers](https://memvers.com/blog/roblox-developer-hiring-guide-2026)) |
| Own game | ≈330 sustained average concurrent users (CCU), or ≈16,000 daily users playing 30 minutes each | ≈$9 of DevEx per average-CCU-month at the platform average (derived below) |
| Creator Store plugin at $29.99 | ≈105–111 sales | 100% of net proceeds, assuming 5–10% goes to tax and processing ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)) |
| FiveM script at €25 on Tebex | ≈150 sales | ~22–23% total fees ([PlayDeck](https://www.playdeck.co/guides/tebex-fees-escrow-selling-scripts/)) |

The currency matters as much as the rate. Clients buy Robux at roughly **$0.0125 each** while the scripter cashes them out at $0.0038, so a Robux payment delivers about 30 cents of every dollar the client spent ([RoHire](https://rohire.dev/blog/understanding-roblox-devex-rates)). Clients who budget in Robux therefore cannot pay expert rates even when they want to; the largest scripting budget RoHire displayed, 60,000 Robux, is worth about $228 after DevEx ([RoHire](https://rohire.dev/)). Robux also arrives slowly. Since 18 June 2026 group payouts land as Pending Robux ([RoUtility News](https://x.com/RoUtilityNews/status/2067676523197268027)), and the cash then waits for the monthly DevEx window. Roblox Wallet, announced at Roblox's 2026 developer conference (RDC), promises payouts every business day, but it reaches only US creators aged 18+ from late 2026, with other countries to follow in 2027 ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)). The working rule is to quote in dollars and, when a client insists on Robux, to price at the DevEx rate of about 263 Robux per dollar.

Two changes in 2026 make income paid through DevEx harder to earn and slower to collect. The first is a shrinking payout pool after mandatory age checks: DevEx fees fell from a **$477 million peak in Q4 2025 to $363 million in Q2 2026** as daily users dropped from 152 million to 123 million ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-bookings-growth-slows-to-8-amid-user-decline-93CH-4826401)), and Roblox cut its 2026 bookings guidance ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/roblox-cuts-2026-guidance-age-123523142.html)). Roblox is steering money toward adults instead, noting that US users over 18 spend about 50% more than younger ones ([Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-bets-on-safety-and-adults-over-user-volume)). The second is a tax change. From **1 November 2026**, DevEx payouts count as royalties, and non-US creators face 0–30% US withholding on the share of earnings that comes from US players. If valid tax information is missing by 31 October, 24% backup withholding applies to the whole payout instead, and Roblox recommends requesting any payout under the old terms by **15 October** ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/tax-information.md)). For a creator in a country with no US tax treaty, the 42% higher rate on US adult spending roughly cancels the 30% withholding on it ($0.0054 × 0.7 ≈ $0.0038), so the 18+ rate protects that creator's income rather than raising it.

## Dollar contracts and system commissions clear the bar soonest

### International-friendly studios pay $3,000 to $6,900 a month

Studio contracts already pay at or above the target. Twin Atlas, maker of Creatures of Sonaria and Dragon Adventures, mixes staff with independent contractors "spread throughout the globe" and pays contract gameplay engineers monthly in USD, "typically **US$3,000–4,000 per month**" ([Built In](https://builtin.com/company/twin-atlas)); it also recruits directly on the DevForum ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/twin-atlas-hiring-gameplay-engineer/4044534)). Creator Games, maker of Anime Card Clash, advertised a remote full-time contract at **$30–40 an hour** with no fixed end date, about $5,200–6,900 a month. It required Luau, Git, Rojo, age 18+ and time-tracking software, and the listing has since closed and probably dates from 2025 ([Work With Indies](https://www.workwithindies.com/careers/creator-games-software-engineer)). Sawhorse Productions lists remote freelance Luau work at $40–60 an hour ([Mediabistro](https://www.mediabistro.com/jobs/3540049378-remote-freelance-roblox-developer-luau-cross-platform)). Among studios that describe themselves as global, Paradoxum Games (Tower Defense Simulator) calls itself an international remote-first studio and lists a content engineer at **$70,000–80,000** ([Paradoxum Games](https://jobs.paradoxum.gg/jobs/content-engineer-full-time/)). The Gang works fully remote across 20 nationalities ([The Gang](https://www.thegang.io/careers)), and Toya and Dubit have recruited remote freelance Roblox programmers ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/toya-hiring-a-programmers-software-engineers/3586613); [gamejobs.co](https://gamejobs.co/Roblox-Developer-Freelancer-Remote-at-Dubit)).

The best-paid roles are restricted by location. Uplift Games (Adopt Me!) pays gameplay engineers $60,000–110,000 or £40,000–65,000, and seniors $110,000–140,000, but hires only in the US and UK ([Uplift Games](https://www.uplift.games/careers/gameplay-engineer); [Uplift Games](https://www.uplift.games/careers/senior-gameplay-engineer-full)). Gamefam's $65,000–105,000 Luau role allowed remote work only from listed US states ([play.vc](https://careers.play.vc/companies/gamefam/jobs/49738927-programmer-iii)). Roblox Corporation has required three office days a week in San Mateo since January 2024 ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/roblox-ceo-tells-remote-workers-to-work-in-person-three-days-a-week-or-lose-their-jobs/1100-6518534/)), and its remote Developer Engagement contract pays $20–25 an hour for creator support rather than engineering ([Built In NYC](https://www.builtinnyc.com/job/developer-engagement-team-contract/6198038)).

Getting hired takes weeks and depends on demonstrating skill live. Uplift's published process runs from application through four interviews, including a 60–90-minute technical session with the hiring manager, and "can take a few weeks" ([Uplift Games](https://www.uplift.games/careers/hiring-process)). Only 17% of Gamefam candidates on Glassdoor rated their interview positively, citing slow feedback and low first offers ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Gamefam-Interview-Questions-E5618910.htm)). Both facts argue for running many applications in parallel and for rehearsing spoken explanations of past architecture. Listings name the same professional toolchain of Rojo, Git and roblox-ts, and studios advertise their own visit and concurrent-player counts ([Work With Indies](https://www.workwithindies.com/careers/creator-games-software-engineer); [Welcome to the Jungle](https://app.welcometothejungle.com/jobs/4G2dziJB)). The strongest application is therefore a public Rojo repository plus credits on live games with numbers attached. Demand is also shifting. Studios that build branded games for advertisers lost work once Roblox began selling ads directly ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/10/roblox-programmatic-ads-independent-studio-brand-introductions/)) and are consolidating ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/roblox-studios-begin-to-consolidate-as-super-league-buys-supersocial/)). Studios with live hits or new funding are the likelier employers, such as Versework, which raised $2.7 million in June 2026 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/versework-asias-largest-roblox-studio-enters-official-roblox-incubator-and-raises-2-7m-from-altos-ventures-302804978.html)). Most posting dates could not be verified, so this evidence shows which pay bands exist, not how many roles are open this month.

### Commissions pay when priced in dollars and sold as systems

Most commission work is found on Discord. HiddenDevs has roughly 250,000–300,000 members ([The Hive Index](https://thehiveindex.com/communities/hiddendevs-roblox/)), RoHire aggregates listings from HiddenDevs and RoDevs in real time ([RoHire](https://rohire.dev/)), and the DevForum's hiring category is carrying 2026 posts again ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/c/collaboration/recruitment/82)). Roblox's own Talent Hub, still discussed on the DevForum after September 2025, draws repeated complaints about low pay, scams and posters who never reply ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/talent-hub-thoughts/3988817)), so it merits a verified profile but little time. The problem is job size. 301 HiddenDevs listings across all roles averaged **$133.64** ([RoPage.gg](https://ropage.gg/blog/how-to-get-your-first-roblox-commissions)), and RoHire puts the average cash payment there at $219 ([RoHire](https://rohire.dev/sources/hidden-devs)). At that size the target takes 14–22 jobs a month, each with its own scoping, scam screening and revision risk.

The money is one tier up. Hiring guides written for Western clients price full combat, inventory or trading systems at **$1,000–3,000** mid-range and $3,000–8,000 at studio quality ([Memvers](https://memvers.com/blog/roblox-developer-hiring-guide-2026)). They put senior systems engineers at $50–120+ an hour and outsourced studio engineers at $4,500–12,000 a month each on retainer ([Game-Ace](https://game-ace.com/blog/how-to-hire-the-best-roblox-game-developer/)). These are vendor figures, not audited transactions, and no verified 2025–26 report shows what full-time commission scripters actually earn per month. Upwork's typical rates are $15–35 an hour ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/roblox-developers/)), so general marketplaces work only for a narrowly specialized offer. Clients most often request server-authoritative combat with anti-exploit, UI, DataStore backends with retries and migrations, inventory and trading, NPC AI, monetization and quests ([Memvers](https://memvers.com/blog/roblox-developer-hiring-guide-2026); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/capable-roblox-scripter-for-hire-%E2%80%93-fast-task-based-solutions-ui-oop-game-systems/3244246)). These systems sit closest to a game's revenue, which justifies $500–1,500 jobs and makes each client a candidate for a monthly retainer to maintain the live game.

Proof that clients can check wins those jobs, and contract terms protect the payment. HiddenDevs' Luau Scripter role requires a working demo place and a 200-line single script linked directly on GitHub ([HiddenDevs](https://www.hiddendevs.com/bulletin?id=2); [HiddenDevs](https://hiddendevs.com/bulletin?id=3)). Applicants must also be the sole credited developer of the work, and review takes up to about 72 hours ([HiddenDevs](https://hiddendevs.com/bulletin?id=4)). Top for-hire posts lead with shipped results such as "560M+ visits, sole programmer" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/for-hire-roblox-scripter-%E2%80%94-560m-visits-sole-programmer-on-shipped-titles/4864122)). On the payment side, PayPal Goods and Services protects the buyer, not the seller ([PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/whats-the-difference-between-friends-and-family-or-goods-and-services-payments-help277)). Documented 2025–26 scams include phishing pages that get past two-factor login, requests to paste code into browser developer tools, and offers to "transfer the group first, payment in 14 days" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/most-common-scams-you-should-be-aware-of/4642306)). The defenses are deposits, milestone payments, recorded proof of working code and keeping the files until paid. Revenue share belongs on top of guaranteed pay, never in place of it: 10% of a game earning 500,000 Robux a month is about $190.

## Own games need about 330 sustained players to pay $3,000

Roblox's own figures show what each player is worth. Creators earned $363 million of DevEx in Q2 2026 against 29 billion engagement hours ([Roblox Q2 2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)), about $0.0125 an hour or **roughly $9 per average concurrent player per month**. A developer rule of thumb of 100 Robux per concurrent player per day gives a similar $11.60 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)). At that average a game needs about **330 sustained average CCU**, or 16,000 daily users playing 30 minutes each, to earn $3,000 a month. The average includes avatar-item sales and is pulled up by top earners, so a competently monetized progression or simulator game should plan on 300–600 CCU and a weakly monetized one on 1,000 or more. Few reach those numbers. Roblox paid creators about $1.7 billion in the year to June 2026, yet the median of its 42,000-plus DevEx participants received about **$1,500** while the top 1,000 averaged $1.4 million ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-dollars65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-dollars1500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html)). Even games with a million or more monthly users keep a median 10.3% of players on day one and 0.5% on day 30, with a 3.8% payer conversion ([GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)). No public data shows what share of new games ever reach 300 CCU, so any plan must budget for several failures.

The 2026 rules reward a specific kind of game. Every creator now needs an age check to publish, and a new game reaches only the 16-and-over age group until it passes an evaluation of 250 highly engaged plays within 60 days. Reaching younger players also requires government ID for adult creators, two-step verification, and either two months of Roblox Plus or Premium or a refundable 1,000-Robux fee ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/kids-and-select.md)). Since June 2026, discovery has ranked games on play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days and playtime over a **28-day window**. It counts only players who arrived organically through the Recommended For You feed and credits at most 60 minutes of play per user per day ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)). The same docs push near-duplicate games down the rankings, and Steal a Brainrot's developers have filed at least four lawsuits against imitators since November 2025 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-24/roblox-s-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)). Together these favour games for older players that hold players over time, use R15 avatars only, and put a distinct twist on a proven game loop. Prices should be fetched at runtime rather than hard-coded, so Roblox Plus discounts display correctly ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus.md)), and paid random items need policy checks and published odds ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/paid-random-items.md)). Roblox backs the same direction with its 2026 Incubator for experienced teams and its rolling Jumpstart program for new games that attract adults ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/03/roblox-announces-incubator-jumpstart-creator-programs)). The hard parts of such a game are engineering problems, which suits a senior scripter.

A hit game is a long shot, and speed is the scripter's edge in improving the odds. Blue Lock: Rivals was built by a 19-year-old in about three months and later sold for $3 million while generating about $5 million a month in player purchases ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/14/indie-game-creators-are-making-millions-selling-their-roblox-worlds/)). Outcomes like that are rare, but they show that build time is not the bottleneck. Testing is cheap. Roblox's ad benchmarks run **$0.0073–0.0187 per play** ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-home-search-combined-new-benchmarks-and-sunsetting-the-classic-flow/4084863)), so $100 buys roughly 5,000–14,000 plays. That is enough to measure first-play bounce and day-one retention against Roblox's benchmark for similar games, whose worked example in the analytics docs shows a median near 12% and a top tenth at 18.7% ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)). Paid plays do not count toward ranking, so ads measure a prototype rather than grow it. Once a game earns, Roblox reports that nearly two in three games putting up to 4% of earnings into ads earned back more than they spent ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)). Creator Rewards adds 5 Robux a day for each qualifying recent spender who plays at least ten minutes ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/creator-rewards.md)), which matters only at scale, and its 60-day hold delays the cash further.

### Products and side streams add hundreds, not thousands

The Creator Store is the best-designed product channel. Sellers keep 100% of net USD proceeds through Stripe, plugins can be priced up to $249.99 and models up to $49.99, and earnings are held for 30 days before paying out on the first of each month ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/creator-store.md)). Model sales opened on 10 December 2025 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/buy-and-sell-models-on-the-creator-store/4142112)). Three limits keep it a supplement: Roblox publishes no actual sales figures, selling is closed in countries Stripe does not support (including Brazil, China, India and Russia), and the ban on obfuscation and remote code loading leaves pure code readable and redistributable by any buyer. Stores outside Roblox cover unsupported countries and higher-priced kits, with BuiltByBit taking a 9.9% platform fee ([BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/creators)).

The remaining streams cannot reach $3,000 for this profile. Open-source sponsorship barely registers, with roblox-ts's maintainer at 5 GitHub sponsors and Fusion's at 1 ([GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/osyrisrblx); [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/dphfox)), so open source works only as marketing. Online Roblox instructors average about **$21.83 an hour** ([ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Online-Roblox-Instructor)), which is steady but far below commission rates. YouTube is slow even at scale; Social Blade estimates AlvinBlox's 453,000-subscriber channel at $281–4,500 a month ([Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/c/alvinblox)). Roblox's HackerOne bug bounty averages $675, $2,875 and $11,100 for medium, high and critical reports ([hacking.community](https://hacking.community/programs/roblox)) and covers Luau memory-safety bugs ([Luau](https://github.com/luau-lang/luau/blob/master/SECURITY.md)), but the large payouts need native-code security skills and arrive unpredictably. Community management tools such as Hyra charge $14.99 a month ([Hyra](https://hyra.io/pricing)), and Roblox OAuth apps are limited to 10 users until they pass review ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud/auth/oauth2-registration.md)). Live-ops and anti-exploit skills therefore earn more as retainers for individual games than as mass-market tools. UGC avatar items pay creators a base 30% of Marketplace sales and are a 3D-art business ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md)).

## Speed pays most on fixed prices and prototype throughput

Ranking the methods against the owner's three assets of depth, speed and full-time hours gives a clear order. Hourly contracts pay for time, so speed shows up only in renewals and raises, but they are the surest route to $3,000. Fixed-price system work turns speed directly into a higher effective hourly rate, and higher rates buy time: a $3,000 floor at $50 an hour needs only 60 billable hours, leaving most of a full-time month for upside work. Own games turn speed into more prototypes per testing dollar, which is the one lever a solo developer controls against long odds. The strongest case for going all-in on games is that one hit out-earns years of contracting, and speed is the scarce input in a market where successful games are copied within weeks ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/how-roblox-theft-is-becoming-a-big-problem)). The case fails on timing. Even a winning prototype pays its first DevEx weeks after launch, and no data shows how many prototypes a winner takes. Products build up slowly, and the remaining streams pay too little or too late.

| 2 | USD fixed-price system commissions, then retainers | $1,000–3,000 mid-range systems; $50–120+/hr senior | Days to weeks | Highest: speed raises the effective rate | The cash engine |
| 3 | Own games (DevEx) | ≈330 sustained CCU for $3,000; median participant ≈$1,500/yr | Months, then 2–6 weeks of payout lag | High skill fit, low odds | Time-boxed upside |
| 4 | Creator Store plugins and models | 100% of net USD; no public sales data | 1.5–3 months | Medium: tooling fits, code unprotectable | Side product that builds slowly |
| 5 | FiveM Lua commissions and Tebex scripts | $30–80/hr; €12–46 scripts at ~22–23% fees | 2–6 weeks to ramp | High: same language and event model | Fallback 1 |
| 6 | TypeScript, Node and Discord-bot freelancing | ~$30/hr TypeScript median; $30–61/hr bots | 2–6 weeks | Medium: roblox-ts carries over | Fallback 2 |

Outside Roblox, FiveM, the GTA V roleplay platform, is the closest match. Its scripts are Lua, and its client/server event model maps onto Roblox RemoteEvents. Its Steam app set a record of **221,298 concurrent players on 3 May 2026** ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/2676230/charts/)). Freelancers there charge about $30–80 an hour ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/hire/fivem)), and roleplay scripts sell for about €12–46 on Tebex stores ([CanX Scripts](https://canx.tebex.io/)), with total fees near 22–23% ([PlayDeck](https://www.playdeck.co/guides/tebex-fees-escrow-selling-scripts/)). The drawback is that Rockstar controls the platform. Its Creator Platform License Agreement, effective 12 January 2026 and revised on 10 September 2026, makes Tebex the only permitted way to take payment ([Cfx.re](https://static.cfx.re/platform-license-agreement-10-sept-2026.pdf)). TypeScript, Node and Discord-bot work is the lower-risk second fallback. ## A four-stage plan reaches $3,000 by about January 2027

The plan orders methods by how quickly they pay and runs a dollar floor alongside a capped bet on upside. Dates assume a start on 5 October 2026 and full-time hours, and every target can be checked against invoices or Creator Hub analytics at month end.

| Stage and window | Core moves | Exit target | Fallback if the target slips |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Setup and proof** (5–18 Oct 2026) | Fill in the profile. ID-verify the Roblox account and turn on two-step verification. File a W-9, or a W-8BEN with a treaty claim, before 31 Oct, and request DevEx before 15 Oct if holding 30,000+ Earned Robux. Open accounts that can receive USD. Record 3–4 system demos of 30–90 seconds each: server-authoritative combat, a data and trading layer with session locking, and monetization with live-ops tooling. Publish a Rojo repo with typed Luau and tests, apply for the HiddenDevs Luau Scripter role, and post a DevForum for-hire thread that leads with shipped results. | Proof package live; 10+ qualified leads listed | No shipped work that can be credited: build one demo place first and cap the stage at three weeks |
| **2 · Cash engine** (weeks 1–9, to early Dec 2026) | Quote fixed USD prices of $500–1,500 per scoped system, with a deposit and milestones. Answer RoHire, HiddenDevs and DevForum leads within hours. Send 8–12 applications (Twin Atlas, Paradoxum, The Gang, Toya, Dubit, Sawhorse, funded studios such as Versework, and DevForum long-term posts; Uplift or Gamefam only if eligible) and rehearse a 60–90-minute live technical interview. | First paid job by week 3; $1,000 invoiced by week 4; $2,000 in month 2; two interview processes active by week 6 | Under $1,000 by week 6: take smaller USD jobs to build references, add a specialized Upwork offer, and start the FiveM ramp |
| **3 · Contracted floor** (months 3–6, Dec 2026–Mar 2027) | Accept a contract at $3,000+ a month or $30+ an hour, or turn the best commission clients into 2–3 monthly retainers for live-ops, optimization or security audits. | First $3,000 month by month 3; $3,000+ contracted or recurring by month 6; no client other than an employer above 50% of income | No offer by month 4: build the floor from retainers plus commissions, add TypeScript or Discord-bot work, and use instructor hours only as a last resort |
| **4 · Upside track** (months 4–12, Jan–Sep 2027; 10–15 hours a week, more if the floor pays a high hourly rate) | Build prototypes in 2–4 weeks each on proven game loops with a twist, aimed at a 16+ audience, R15-only, server-authoritative, with policy-checked monetization. Buy about $100 of plays per test. Ship one Creator Store plugin, or a kit sold off-platform where Stripe is unavailable. | 3–4 prototypes tested by month 9; one with day-one retention of 12%+ and low first-play bounce; one game holding 100+ average daily users for 60 days | Four prototypes below benchmark: stop the game track and move the hours to products and retainers, or join a funded team |
| **Checkpoint** (month 9 onward) | Scale only when organic players perform and ads earn back more than they cost, putting up to 4% of earnings into ads. | Shift contract hours to a game only in proportion to the cash it paid in two consecutive DevEx cycles | Otherwise, negotiate toward senior rates ($50+ an hour) and keep selling products |

Each trigger guards against a specific risk. The week-four and week-six checks test early whether this portfolio can win dollar jobs of $500 or more, which leaves time to switch to FiveM, estimated at two to six weeks to learn. The 50% client cap guards against the most common contractor failure: one client ending. The game track waits for four failed tests rather than one because there is no data on how often new games succeed. The checkpoint waits for two DevEx cycles because DevEx cash arrives weeks after the earnings, and Creator Rewards 60 days after. The type of floor also decides the time left for upside work. A full-time contract paying $3,000–4,000 a month meets the target but leaves only evenings, while $3,000 of retainers at $50 an hour needs about 60 hours a month and frees more than 100 hours for games and products. An owner who prefers steady pay should take a contract at $30–40 an hour, which beats the target by roughly $2,200–3,900 a month. An owner who wants upside should push for fewer, higher-rate retainers.

Three blank profile fields change the plan, so filling them in is the first task. Country decides eligibility for US- or UK-only roles such as Uplift and Gamefam, access to Creator Store selling through Stripe, and the treaty rate on DevEx withholding. Age matters because contracts like Creator Games' require 18+, minors need a parent's agreement to sell on the Creator Store, and adult creators need government ID to reach players under 16. A portfolio of shipped games with visit and concurrent-player numbers should lead every pitch; without one, the first stage stretches to build a demo place. Throughout, the plan stays inside Roblox's rules and the law. It excludes exploit executors, account or item sales, Robux reselling, gambling mechanics and leaked assets. Anti-exploit work is sold only as defensive audits, and art and assets for own games are commissioned with a written transfer of rights, which also keeps a successful game sellable.

## Conclusion

The real constraint on $3,000 a month is currency and the supply of jobs, not skill or hourly rate. At full-time hours the target works out to less per hour than an online Roblox instructor earns on average. A senior scripter who prices in dollars and sells revenue-related systems to funded studios therefore faces an easier problem than creator-earnings medians suggest, while one who bets everything on a single own game faces a far harder one. The decision that matters is the order of the steps, not the choice of one method: the skills that win a contract also build the prototypes, and the contract pays for the tests.

The 2026 rule changes cut both ways for this profile. Age-gated publishing, the 28-day discovery window, the 18+ rate and policy checks on monetization reward games for older players that keep them coming back, and the hard parts of those games (economy, data persistence, server authority and compliance) suit a senior scripter. The same changes also shrink the audience a new game reaches first, and for non-US creators they add US withholding from November that the 18+ rate only offsets. The sensible response is to keep options open: a dollar floor first, then many cheap, time-boxed tests. Three developments would justify revisiting the plan: the completed profile, a prototype that clears the day-one retention gate, or a contract offer above $30 an hour, which would make the contract the core of the plan and any game a side project.
